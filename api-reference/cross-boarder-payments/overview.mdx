The **Cross-Border Payments API** lets you send international payouts on behalf of your users.
Every endpoint is exposed under the `/api/platform/cross-border/` namespace and follows a
predictable flow: look up reference data, set up the payee, create the payout, then track its status.

You can use these endpoints to:

- Look up supported payout **countries**, **banks**, and live **exchange rates**
- Read **payer rules** so you collect the mandatory fields for each corridor
- Save and manage a payee's **addresses** and **bank accounts**
- **Create** a cross-border payout and **track** its status

---

## How Cross-Border Payouts Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load reference data">
    Fetch the supported [payout countries](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/list-payout-countries),
    [payout banks](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/list-payout-banks), and current
    [exchange rates](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/get-exchange-rates) for the destination.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check payer rules">
    Call [Get Payer Rules](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/get-payer-rules) to discover the
    mandatory fields required for the selected corridor before you collect payee details.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up the payee">
    Save the recipient's [address](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/add-payee-address) and
    [bank account](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/add-payee-bank) for the user. These are
    reusable across future payouts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the payout">
    Submit the transfer with
    [Create a Cross-Border Payout](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/create-cross-border-payout).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track the status">
    Poll [Get Cross-Border Payout Status](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/get-cross-border-payout-status)
    or listen for webhooks to follow the payout through to completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Payee addresses and banks are stored **per user** (`user_id`). Set them up once and reuse them for
  every subsequent payout to that recipient.
</Info>

## Best Practices

- Always read **[Payer Rules](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/get-payer-rules)** for the target
  corridor before collecting payee details — required fields vary by country and bank.
- Fetch **[Exchange Rates](/api-reference/cross-boarder-payments/get-exchange-rates)** close to payout
  time so quoted amounts reflect current rates.
- Reuse saved payee **addresses** and **banks** instead of recreating them for every payout.
- Track payouts with the **status** endpoint or webhooks rather than assuming success on creation.
